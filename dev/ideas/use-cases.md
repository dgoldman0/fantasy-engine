# Use cases

What people might do with Fantasy Engine beyond what the plan already
covers. The plan's five Practices, Everyday, Workshop, Study, Publishing and
Investigation, are decided (decision 26) and described in Part 6 of
[PLAN.md](../PLAN.md). This file collects uses that aren't yet part of any
Practice or plan. Nothing here is decided.

## Starting points already on record

- **The Detective Box's wider uses.** Investigation is the Detective Box
  idea as a Practice. The idea's original notes, kept as `IDEA.md` and
  `DETECTIVE-BOX.md` under the tag `archive/chimera-a499082-20261027`, name
  three uses: incident response and threat hunting, compliance, audit and
  forensics, and large-scale corpus intelligence. They also cast the machine
  as a tool that AI agents call for verified facts, timelines and graphs,
  each returned with receipts and steps to replay it. Investigation as
  planned covers evidence, a timeline, a graph and linked findings. The
  corpus work and the agent-facing tool go further, and either could become
  its own Practice or part of one.
- **Kinds of world.** The July applet note lists peer world types for Worlds:
  facility and supply-chain models, network-failure models and cyber ranges,
  emergency exercises, ecology and epidemic models, digital twins,
  multi-agent evaluation, historical and educational systems, and fictional
  worlds ([applet-concepts.md](applet-concepts.md), section 4.5). Any of them
  could anchor a Practice.
