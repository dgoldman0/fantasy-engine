# After the first release

Ideas for the releases that follow 0.1.0. Nothing here is decided, except
what the plan has already placed after the alpha.

## Placed after the alpha by the plan

- Investigation, the fifth Practice (decision 26, Part 6 of
  [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md)).
- macOS (decision 23).
- Sound Lab, Worlds and Observatory, along with audio output and the
  rich-terminal series path (decision 35).
- A fully authentic profiler for MegaPad and LM1: timing models calibrated
  against each chip's RTL, held to its clocks on the bench programs within
  a stated margin, in the second release (decision 39).

## Directions recorded in other idea files

- The filesystem derived from ext4 that would follow standard ext4
  ([native-filesystem.md](native-filesystem.md)).
- MediaLab, as SoundLab's possible growth, and the other directions in
  section 6 of [applet-concepts.md](applet-concepts.md).
- The language beyond what the first release takes: running lists that a
  program builds as it runs (decision 32, [language.md](language.md)).
- More uses of score-guided search: Monte Carlo tree search for games, Pad's
  spelling corrections, Library's ranked search and Streams' ranking of feed
  items (Akashic's `docs/search/scored-search.md`).
