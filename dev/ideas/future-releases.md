Ideas for the releases that follow 0.1.0. Nothing here is decided, except
what the plan has already placed after the alpha.

## Placed after the alpha by the plan

- Investigation, the fifth Practice (decision 26, Part 6 of
  [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md)).
- macOS (decision 23).
- Sound Lab, Worlds and Observatory, along with audio output and the
  rich-terminal series path (decision 35).

  ([native-filesystem.md](native-filesystem.md)).
- MediaLab, as SoundLab's possible growth, and the other directions in

  running lists built while a program runs (decision 32,
